# My Favorite Movie

## Hacksaw Ridge (2016)

**Hacksaw Ridge** is my favorite movie. It is about a soldier named Desmond Doss who joins the army during World War II but refuses to carry a gun because of his beliefs.

### Main Character
- **Desmond Doss** – A soldier who saves wounded men without using a weapon.

### Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it shows courage, faith, and the importance of saving lives. It also teaches us to stand by our beliefs even when things get difficult.

### My Favorite Parts
1. The war scenes.
2. Desmond helping wounded soldiers.
3. His courage and determination.

### My Rating
**10/10** – A great movie with an inspiring story.

> "You can still make a difference without using a weapon."

**Genre:** War, Drama, Biography

**Release Year:** 2016
